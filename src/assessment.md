## About the unit assessments

### What are the assessment items, when are they due, and what are they worth?

The assessment for {{ siteinfo.unitcode }} consists of
two quizzes, a group project, and a final examination.

- Quiz 1: available and due in week 4 (worth 7.5%)
- Quiz 2: completed in-class in labs, in week 7 (worth 7.5%)
- Project: a group project with multiple deliverables, completed in weeks 4--12 (worth
  35%).\
  The phase 1 project brief, listing the deliverables and due dates, is now available
  on the Blackboard LMS announcements forum, and on Moodle.
- Exam: a face-to-face exam, held during the examinations period (worth 50%).
